The lore

How a game username became a tech brand.

  1. Age 11

    A kid needs a username.

    I mashed some letters together for a video game account. "WizMinar" was random, but unique enough to not be taken.

    what does it mean?

    no idea. still don't.

  2. Jul 2020

    The channel goes live.

    WizMinar on YouTube: gaming, unscripted moments and a lot of chaos, with a guy as clueless about his own brand as you are.

  3. 2021

    First websites.

    A games page, a fake airline, a travel site, a fashion blog. Rough HTML and CSS, but they worked.

  4. 2026

    IIT Kanpur, batch one.

    I joined the first-ever B.Cyber batch: about 30 people and no seniors to follow. We sat down with the Director and C3iHub, then got told to draw the path ourselves.

  5. Now

    The name finally has a job.

    WizMinar is my tech brand now: security tools, systems, Linux and videos about what I'm learning. I still don't know what the name means, and that's half the fun.
